And the reach that follows them

Every episode puts our content in front of the guest’s following as well as our own. The Arkells trailer reached 38,694 views across coordinated uploads. Band-official accounts including @arkellsmusic, @thetrews and @meligroveband now republish or co-sign directly. A partner’s credit travels with it: when a band reposts an episode, the partner’s name lands on the band’s own grid, in front of an audience no media buy reaches.

Micro-content

One episode. Two weeks of content. Every platform.

Each episode is cut into two to four short clips that run on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Facebook. That multi-platform engine is behind the 699K views, and it is why a partner’s name keeps showing up long after release night. The Tim Oxford clip below reached 24.7K views on Instagram alone.

The double-drum session

1 song.2 drummers.360 degrees.

Every episode ends with two drummers playing one of the guest’s songs together, filmed in 360°. You sit right behind the drums and look anywhere in the room.

The contest

Where the ad spend goes.

Every episode cycle runs a giveaway: signed sticks and setlists from the guest, the winner drawn on camera and announced in the next episode. Three of every four ad dollars we spend sit behind the contest reels, so a partner on the contest shows up in paid and organic reach in the same post.

Who is watching

A concentrated audience, not a crowd.

The demographic, for the media planner in the room. Most programming reaches everyone and hopes your customer is somewhere in it. This one reaches one audience, all the way through.

82%MenAgainst a 46% Canadian Meta baseline — an over-index of 36 points.
85%Aged 25–54Prime earning years, peaking in the 35–44 bracket.
65%CanadianWith a further 23% in the United States, and Toronto the single largest city.
97.6%Positive sentimentMeasured across YouTube comments. An audience that argues about ghost notes, not about anything a brand would need to distance itself from.

This is not a general music audience. It is a loyal, concentrated one, at the age most brands pay a premium to reach.
