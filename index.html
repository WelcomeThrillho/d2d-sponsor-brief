Working together

Five ways a brand fits in.

These are formats the show already produces every cycle — a partner steps into them rather than commissioning something untested.

01

The double-drum session

Two kits, two drummers, one song, filmed in 360° so the viewer can look anywhere in the room. Nothing else in drum media looks like this, and every piece of gear in the room is on camera from every angle.

02

Spec and setup segments

A walkthrough of what the guest plays and why. The YC Drum Company post is the template — it earned 5,037 organic views with no paid support, because kit detail is the reason this audience is here.

03

Contest prizing

Every cycle runs a giveaway — signed sticks and a shirt — and it is consistently our strongest follower-conversion mechanic, at up to 114 new followers from a single post. A partner supplying product takes that entire spike.

04

Release-week amplification

Every episode gets a paid push on release week, already running and already efficient at $0.61 per follower. Partner-integrated episodes inherit that machinery rather than starting from zero.

05

Craft and technique reels

The highest-saved format on the channel. A brand’s story — how a head is made, why a cymbal is lathed a certain way — belongs here, where the audience already treats this content as reference material.
