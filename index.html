The trajectory

The audience has nearly tripled this year.

Nine months of biweekly releases, 13 episodes, and two step-changes in growth — both of them strategy rather than luck. March, when the show went to several reels per episode. August, when stronger guest cycles and release-night boosts produced the biggest month in its history.

3xMarch over FebruaryGoing from one supporting reel per episode to two to four tripled monthly views inside a month.

Both jumps came from a change in how the show is made and promoted, so Season 5 starts from the top of the curve, not the bottom.

Working together

Five ways a partner shows up.

These are formats the show already produces every cycle — a partner steps into them rather than commissioning something untested. None of them assumes the partner makes drums.

01

The sponsor spot

A six-second spot between the show open and the episode itself, professionally shot by our crew during the season’s production, the way we made Sennheiser’s. It runs at the top of every episode in the season, everywhere the episode goes.

02

The double-drum session

Two kits, two drummers, one song, filmed in 360° so the viewer can look anywhere in the room. Nothing else in music media looks quite like this, and everything in the room is on camera from every angle.

03

Your story, told the way we tell theirs

The highest-saved format on the channel is a guest breaking down how a part actually works. The same treatment applied to a partner — the people, the craft, why it is done that way — is the most natural place for a brand to start. Every company has someone in the back hitting things.

04

Contest prizing

Every cycle runs a giveaway, and it is consistently our strongest follower-conversion mechanic, at up to 114 new followers from a single post. The prize does not have to be gear: a partner-supplied prize or experience takes that entire spike, with the partner’s name on the entry.

05

Release-week amplification

Every episode gets a paid push on release week, already running and already efficient. Partner-integrated episodes inherit that machinery rather than starting from zero.
