The contest

Where the ad spend goes.

Every episode cycle runs a giveaway: signed sticks and setlists from the guest, the winner drawn on camera and announced in the next episode. Three of every four ad dollars we spend sit behind the contest reels, so a partner on the contest shows up in paid and organic reach in the same post.
